The 4-Layer Engineering Manifesto

Standard sneakers use cheap open-cell foam that packs out after 3 months. URBANZA develops custom nitrogen-infused micro-cellular midsoles that retain 96% elasticity over 800+ kilometers.

1. Hydro-Weave Monofilament

Sheds rainwater while allowing perspiration vapor to escape freely.

2. ArchFlex Stabilizer Shank

Thermoplastic polyurethane plate embedded to control torsional twisting.

3. Dual-Density Nitrogen Foam

Firm on the perimeter for cornering stability, plush in the center for cloud-like cushioning.

4. Vulcanised High-Tack Rubber

65-durometer rubber tested extensively on slick London tube station tile floors.

Born On London Pavements.

Why We Built URBANZA

In 2022, we noticed a broken cycle in footwear: multinational conglomerates were charging £220+ for plastic sneakers produced in mass factories for less than £14.

URBANZA was founded in an East London garage to invert this model. We invest 80% of our production budget into supercritical foam compounding, Italian full-grain hides, and recycled nylon knits — selling directly to the street with zero middleman markups.

Every pair is tested over 200 miles across wet autumn sidewalks, cobblestone alleyways, and nighttime tube stations before it receives the URBANZA stamp.
